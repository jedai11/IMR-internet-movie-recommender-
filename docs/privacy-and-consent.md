# IMR (Internet Movie Recommender) Privacy, Data Minimization & Transparency Principles

## 1. Transparency Framework (Informed by AI Ethics & Article 50 Standards)
IMR analyzes voluntary user input to infer emotional states solely for entertainment and cinematic matching. Designed with transparency and data minimization at its core:
- Users are clearly informed that an emotion-matching system is active before and during their session.
- IMR displays an explicit, persistent disclosure banner prior to recommendation execution.
- Recommendation rationales and mathematical affect distances are fully visible on every recommended title.

## 2. Sensor-Free Architecture
- IMR does **NOT** use webcams, facial emotion recognition (FER), microphones, vocal acoustic analysis, or wearable biometric monitors.
- All affective coordinates are derived from:
  1. Direct, explicit user placement on the 2D Russell Circumplex canvas.
  2. Voluntary free-text emotional reflection provided by the user.

## 3. Data Minimization & Pseudonymous Sessions
- **Pseudonymous Sessions:** User identity is represented by random client-generated session keys (`sess_anon_...`). No email, phone number, name, or account registration is collected or required.
- **Data Erasure:** Users can erase all session logs, mood inputs, and movie feedback at any time via the 1-click button in the Consent & Transparency Center.
